## What's new in 0.2.0

### Playback
- **Fair queue** – songs take turns between people, so someone who adds five songs doesn't jump ahead of everyone else. Admin priority still comes first. Can be turned off in **Admin → Rules**.
- **Crossfade** – the end of a song smoothly blends into the next one. Off by default; turn it on and set the length (1–15 s) in **Admin → Rules**.
- **Volume by time** – zones like "Quiet hours 22:00–07:00 → 30 %". A zone can lower or raise the volume and can span midnight. The admin sees which zone is active next to the volume slider.
- **Even out the volume** – every song is measured once and played louder or quieter (with a limiter), so a quiet old recording and a loud modern master end up at a similar level. On by default, **Admin → System**.
- **Several output devices** – tick more devices in **Admin → System** and the music plays on all of them at once (best for different rooms).
- Songs added while something is playing are now downloaded and prepared right away, not just before they play.

### DJs
- **What DJs can add** – allow or block YouTube Music search, YouTube / Spotify links and the local library separately in **Admin → Rules** (e.g. only music from the disk). Admins can always add everything.

### Fixes
- **Radio:** song titles sent in Windows-1250 / ISO-8859-2 or with HTML entities (`&#353;`) are now shown correctly instead of `�`.
- **Display:** long song titles shrink until they fit instead of being cut off.
